# Third-party assets

## Piece images

`decodex/static/pieces/*.svg` is the **cburnett** chess piece set, as shipped by
lichess in `lichess-org/lila` under `public/piece/cburnett/`.

- Author: Colin M.L. Burnett
- Licence: CC-BY-SA 3.0 <https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/>
- Source: <https://github.com/lichess-org/lila/tree/master/public/piece/cburnett>

The files are unmodified. CC-BY-SA covers the images only; it does not extend to
the rest of this project.
